Part 1 Introduction to Semiconductors

1 The Semiconductor Value Chain

Read from left to right, this is the path a chip takes from design to the customer who uses it. Each cell lists only the companies I consider most representative and instantly recognizable, so some cells hold a single company or are empty. The third row lists representative companies from other countries (the US, the Netherlands, Japan and South Korea). It is not a ranking.

Taiwanese foundries (TSMC) take orders from IC design companies. Some companies do both, and are called IDMs (Integrated Device Manufacturers), for example Infineon. Equipment makers (such as ZEISS and TRUMPF) sit in the "Equipment" column, upstream of the whole chain.
